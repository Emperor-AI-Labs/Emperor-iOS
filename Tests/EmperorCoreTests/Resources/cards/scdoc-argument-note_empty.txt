Prepare a complete, court-ready argument note. Open with a short synopsis and the questions that arise, then argue ISSUE BY ISSUE — for each issue set out: the proposition (of law or fact); the submission with its reasoning; the authorities relied on (pin-cited, each tagged with its good-law status); the references to the record (page / paragraph / exhibit); and the opponent's likely answer with the rebuttal to it. Close with the relief / prayer. Written in the register of arguing counsel — forceful yet measured, never overstating; where the law is unsettled, say so, and never fabricate a citation. Write in the measured register of senior counsel. Cite authorities with their current standing [Settled] / [Doubted] / [Overruled] / [Pending]; do not fabricate a citation — a candid gap is preferable. Where the answer turns on the client's instructions or on a matter for the court's discretion, flag it with a bracketed note.
